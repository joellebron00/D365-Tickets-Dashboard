WORKPLACE CONCERNS ANALYTICS
Requirements from the brief vs. what the Desk365 export supports today
=====================================================================

Source: Desk365 ticket-list export (Tkt_Lst_Created_Time_<Mon>_01_<yyyy>_to_<Mon>_31_<yyyy>.csv).
Status legend: AVAILABLE = covered with the current export | PROXY / PARTIAL = covered indirectly
or in part | GAP = not possible until new data is captured.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Trends by concern category and subcategory
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Status:           AVAILABLE
Today:            Category / SubCategory come in the export. The dashboard shows tickets per
                  week by category, a drill-down into subcategories, and the top category >
                  subcategory pairs (Overview and Categories & themes tabs).
What to add:      An ER-specific branch in the taxonomy. Today "HR - Policy & General HR > Other"
                  is a catch-all, so most Workplace Concerns are not distinguishable from general
                  HR requests. Category and SubCategory are assigned at close, not at intake, so
                  open cases are uncategorised; make them required at intake for ER cases.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Repeat themes tied to policies, processes, programs or manager practices
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Status:           PROXY / PARTIAL
Today:            Rule-based theme tags read the subject and description of each ticket and
                  surface recurring topics (I-9 / work authorization, ADP / timekeeping, pay,
                  manager / leadership, conduct / complaint language, etc.) independently of
                  the agent's categorisation. A conduct-language watch list flags tickets to
                  review. The Overview counts repeat requesters (2+ tickets).
What to add:      Structured fields captured at intake / review: Concern type, Related policy or
                  process, Manager involved (Y/N), and Root cause (set at close). Text mining is a
                  second lens, not a replacement for these fields.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
3. Volume by business area, department, location or client program
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Status:           PROXY / PARTIAL
Today:            Client Program is populated in the export (~94% of tickets) and is used in the
                  heat maps and filters. The export has NO employee department, location, state,
                  business area or manager: the "Department", "Direct Manager" and "Job Title"
                  form fields come out empty.
What to add:      Capture Department, Location / State and Business Area on the Workplace
                  Concerns form (dropdowns), or join the tickets to an employee roster by email
                  (ADP / Sage) in a later phase.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
4. Time to intake, review and resolution
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Status:           AVAILABLE
Today:            Created -> First Assigned (intake), Created -> First Replied (review) and
                  Created -> Resolved (resolution), shown in business hours (Desk365 clock,
                  Mon-Fri 08:00-18:00) and in calendar hours (what the employee experiences).
                  The Timeliness tab also shows where the clock runs (Open = handling vs
                  Pending = waiting) and the aging of the active backlog.
What to add:      Agree what intake, review and resolution mean for a concern (e.g. intake =
                  acknowledged, review = investigation started, resolution = employee informed)
                  and add status values or date fields that match those stages.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
5. Escalation trends
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Status:           GAP
Today:            Desk365 has no escalation field. Priority Urgent / High is reported as what it
                  is (the share of tickets with Urgent or High priority), not as an escalation
                  measure.
What to add:      "Escalated to" (None / HR Lead / Legal / Leadership / Client) and
                  "Escalation date" fields, or an "Escalated" status, so escalations can be
                  counted and trended.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
6. Heat maps of recurring concerns / hotspots
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Status:           PROXY / PARTIAL
Today:            Heat maps of category x client program, category x requester type,
                  category x assigned agent, intake timing (weekday x hour) and agent x
                  category (Hotspots and Workload tabs).
What to add:      Manager, location and department heat maps need the fields in item 3. Keep a
                  minimum cell size (e.g. 3 tickets) when reporting on small groups to protect
                  confidentiality.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
7. Identification of training, communication, policy or process improvement opportunities
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Status:           PROXY / PARTIAL
Today:            The theme x category heat map shows where generic categories hide specific
                  topics; the handling-vs-waiting analysis shows where tickets wait; the
                  per-category SLA view shows where the process struggles.
What to add:      An "Outcome / resolution code" at close (coaching, training, policy update,
                  process fix, no action, referred...) and a "Root cause" field, so improvements
                  can be measured rather than inferred.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
8. Monthly trend reporting, especially during TTL ramp-ups
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Status:           PROXY / PARTIAL
Today:            The dashboard reads the most recent monthly export from the shared Drive
                  folder automatically and reports the month by day and by week. Month-over-
                  month comparison is not shown because only the latest file is read.
What to add:      Keep every monthly export in the Drive folder with the standard name and
                  date range (one calendar month per file). Reading all files, de-duplicated by
                  Ticket Number, would enable month-over-month trends without any change in
                  Desk365. Later, automate the feed with the Desk365 API or Power BI.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
9. Confidentiality, visibility and accountability
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Status:           AVAILABLE
Today:            Requester names and emails are never shown (a masked ID is used internally);
                  ticket descriptions are only scanned for themes and never displayed; subject
                  lines are not displayed; workload is shown per agent as capacity, not as a
                  ranking. The app is private on Streamlit Community Cloud and reads from a
                  Drive folder shared only with a service account.
What to add:      Agree an access model: who sees case detail (Desk365) vs. aggregates (this
                  dashboard). Consider a separate, restricted Desk365 group for ER cases.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
Notes on the export itself
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- All "* Duration (mins)" and "* Business Hours (mins)" columns are BUSINESS minutes
  (Mon-Fri 08:00-18:00, 600 min per day). "Open (mins)" and "Pending (mins)" are calendar
  minutes. The dashboard shows both views.
- "Completed Within SLA" is the only SLA field; the SLA targets themselves are configured in
  Desk365 and are not part of the export. Document the targets per priority.
- Voicemail transcriptions arrive as tickets from the HR call queue without a usable
  requester identity and are usually filed under a generic category.
- Spam / non-actionable tickets, automatic replies and vendor / system notifications are
  excluded from every number in the dashboard.
